Added a full band AM/FM station scan with a saved, sorted list of up to 256 stations per band and live scan results in the left panel. The Stations menu has Clear Scan, Append Scan, and Clear entries; hold Clear Scan to replace the list or Append Scan to add newly found frequencies. Stopping a scan saves its progress. Hold a saved frequency or Clear twice to confirm deletion. Previously saved 128-station lists need to be rescanned. Regular encoder tuning now defaults to saved Stations; choose Saved or Step in the main menu's Tune submenu.
